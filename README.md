# 📘 Multi-Factor Trend & Reversal Confirmation System (MTRCS)

This document provides a detailed explanation of all components of the **Multi-Factor Trend & Reversal Confirmation System (MTRCS)** — a technical indicator designed to identify trend direction, potential reversals, and key market levels using a combination of price action, moving averages, RSI, and support/resistance analysis.

---

## 🔵 Moving Average (Blue Line)

- A **20-period Simple Moving Average (SMA)** representing the average closing price over the last 20 candles.
- **Purpose**: Identifies the general direction of the market.
  - **Price above** the blue line → signals a potential **uptrend**.
  - **Price below** the blue line → signals a potential **downtrend**.

---

## 🔺 Green & 🔻 Red Arrows

- **Green Upward Triangle (▲)**: Appears **below** the candle to indicate the start of a potential **uptrend**.
- **Red Downward Triangle (▼)**: Appears **above** the candle to indicate the start of a potential **downtrend**.

### 📌 Conditions for Signal Appearance:
- Price is **above or below** the moving average.
- RSI is **above or below 50**.
- Price is **higher or lower** than the previous candle's close.

---

## 📉 Support & Resistance Lines

- **Green Dashed Lines**: Represent **support levels**, where buying interest may occur.
- **Red Dashed Lines**: Represent **resistance levels**, where selling pressure may appear.
- These zones are derived from the **highest and lowest prices over the past 50 candles**.

---

## 📊 RSI (Relative Strength Index) Display

Displays the current **RSI value**, providing insight into market momentum and possible extremes:
- **RSI > 70**: Market may be **overbought** (shown in **red**).
- **RSI < 30**: Market may be **oversold** (shown in **green**).
- **RSI between 30–70**: Market is in a **neutral range** (shown in **white**).

---

## 🧾 Information Table (Top Right Corner)

Provides two essential metrics at a glance:
- **Current Market Trend**:
  - "Uptrend" 🟢
  - "Downtrend" 🔴
  - "Sideways" ⚪️
- **RSI Value**: Displays the current RSI level with color-coded interpretation.

---

## ✅ How to Use MTRCS Effectively

### 🔄 Trend-Following Strategy
- Look for **green arrows (▲)** during uptrends for potential **long entries**.
- Look for **red arrows (▼)** during downtrends for potential **short entries**.

### 📍 Support & Resistance Interaction
- Monitor price behavior at **green (support)** and **red (resistance)** lines.
- These zones can be used to set **entry/exit** points or **stop-loss** levels.

### 📈 RSI as Confirmation
- Use RSI values to **validate trend strength**.
- **Extreme RSI readings** may suggest potential **trend exhaustion or reversals**.

---

## 💡 Optimal Signal Conditions

The most reliable trade setups occur when:
- Price is **above** the blue moving average.
- RSI is **above 50**.
- A **green arrow (▲)** is displayed.
- Price is approaching or near a **support level**.

---

Always combine this system with proper risk management and broader market context for best results. ⚖️📊
